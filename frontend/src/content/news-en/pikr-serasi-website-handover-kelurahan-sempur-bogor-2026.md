**STMIK Tazkia** student officially delivered the **PIK-R SERASI** (Pusat Informasi dan Konseling Remaja — Youth Information and Counseling Center, Sempur Healthy and Inspirational Youth) website to **PIK-R SERASI Kelurahan Sempur, Central Bogor Sub-district**. This project was developed by **Shanaya Balghis Riyona**, a [Computer Engineering](/en/programs/computer-engineering) student, as part of a community service project supervised by **Hairul Umam, S.Si., M.Si.**, Head of the Computer Engineering Program at STMIK Tazkia.

The website can be accessed at [https://pik-r-serasi.vercel.app/](https://pik-r-serasi.vercel.app/).

![PIK-R SERASI website handover to Kelurahan Sempur Bogor by STMIK Tazkia student](/images/news/penyerahan-website-pikr-serasi-kelurahan-sempur-01.jpeg)

## Project Background

PIK-R (Youth Information and Counseling Center) is a national program aimed at providing information and counseling services for youth regarding reproductive health, family life planning, and personal development. Kelurahan Sempur, a sub-district in the city of Bogor, runs the PIK-R SERASI program which had previously relied on conventional media for information dissemination.

Recognizing the need for digitalization, STMIK Tazkia initiated the development of the PIK-R SERASI website through its community service program. The website is expected to serve as a digital platform that extends the reach of information and counseling services to youth in the Kelurahan Sempur area and surrounding communities.

## Website Development by Student

The PIK-R SERASI website was developed by **Shanaya Balghis Riyona**, a Computer Engineering student at STMIK Tazkia, under the direct supervision of **Hairul Umam, S.Si., M.Si.** The development process included several stages:

### Requirements Analysis

The student team conducted surveys and interviews with PIK-R SERASI administrators and Kelurahan Sempur officials to understand the information needs and required features. The analysis results formed the basis for designing a website tailored to user needs.

### Design and Development

Based on the analysis, the student designed and built the website with features supporting PIK-R SERASI activities:

- **PIK-R SERASI profile** — complete information about the organization, vision and mission, and organizational structure
- **Activity information** — schedules and documentation of youth education and counseling activities
- **Educational content publication** — articles and educational materials on youth health and personal development
- **Documentation gallery** — photos and videos of PIK-R SERASI activities
- **Contact information** — communication channels for consultation and inquiries

### Testing and Training

Before handover, the website underwent testing to ensure proper functionality. The student team also provided training to PIK-R SERASI administrators and Kelurahan Sempur staff on how to manage and update website content.

![Documentation of PIK-R SERASI website handover and training activities](/images/news/penyerahan-website-pikr-serasi-kelurahan-sempur-02.jpeg)
