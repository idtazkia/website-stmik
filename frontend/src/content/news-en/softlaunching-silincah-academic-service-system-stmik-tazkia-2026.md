**STMIK Tazkia** officially introduced the **SILINCAH (Sistem Integrasi Layanan Campus Akademik Handal — Integrated Reliable Campus Academic Service System)** application through a soft launching event held at the STMIK Tazkia campus in Bogor. The application was launched by STMIK Tazkia Chairman **Miftakhus Surur, S.E.I., M.Sc (Fin)., AWPS** together with Vice Chairman II **Galih Kurniawan Sidik, M.Kom.** and the Heads of Study Programs, namely Head of Computer Engineering Program **Hairul Umam, S.Si., M.Si.** and Head of Information Systems Program **Endy Muardin, S.T., M.E.** The event was attended by all **KIP-Kuliah (Kartu Indonesia Pintar Kuliah — Indonesia Smart Card for Higher Education)** scholarship students as early adopters of the application.

![SILINCAH Logo — Integrated Reliable Campus Academic Service System](/images/news/logo-silincah-stmik-tazkia.png)

## About the SILINCAH Application

**SILINCAH** is an integrated digital platform designed to unify all campus academic services into a single reliable, efficient, and accessible system. The application can be accessed at [https://silincah.stmik.tazkia.ac.id](https://silincah.stmik.tazkia.ac.id/login). It was developed as part of STMIK Tazkia's commitment to digital transformation in higher education services.

### Key Features of SILINCAH

The SILINCAH application provides a range of integrated academic service features:

- **Centralized academic information** — access to class schedules, grades, and academic data in one platform
- **Digital administrative services** — online letter requests, leave applications, and academic administration
- **Study progress monitoring** — real-time tracking of credit hours, GPA, and graduation status
- **Integrated communication** — direct communication channels between students, lecturers, and the academic office
- **Notifications and reminders** — automatic alerts for schedules, deadlines, and important campus information

## Soft Launching with KIP-Kuliah Students

The SILINCAH soft launching specifically involved **KIP-Kuliah** scholarship students as early adopters. The selection of KIP-Kuliah students as the first group to use the application reflects the campus's commitment to ensuring easy access to academic services for all students, particularly those receiving government educational assistance.

![Soft launching atmosphere of the SILINCAH application with KIP-Kuliah scholarship students at STMIK Tazkia](/images/news/softlaunching-silincah-stmik-tazkia-kip-kuliah-2026.jpeg)

During the event, students received a hands-on introduction to SILINCAH's features along with a usage guide. Feedback and input from these early users will serve as the foundation for refining the application before its full launch to the entire STMIK Tazkia academic community.

## Benefits of Academic Service Digitalization

The implementation of SILINCAH is expected to have a positive impact on the entire academic community:

- **Time efficiency** — students no longer need to handle administration manually or visit the academic office in person
- **Information transparency** — all academic data is accessible in real-time and with accuracy
- **Ease of access** — academic services are available anytime and anywhere through digital devices
- **Paper reduction** — supporting an eco-friendly campus through document and process digitalization
