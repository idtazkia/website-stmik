## About the EC-Council C|AIPM Certification

The **Certified AI Program Manager (C|AIPM)** is a professional certification from EC-Council designed to equip participants with the skills to lead, manage, and govern AI initiatives comprehensively. The certification covers:

- **AI fundamentals** — understanding of AI concepts, machine learning, and deep learning
- **AI governance and management** — strategies for implementing AI within organizations
- **AI risk management** — identifying and mitigating risks in AI projects
- **Data-driven decision making** — leveraging data to support strategic decisions
- **Stakeholder communication** — conveying technical AI concepts to non-technical audiences

This internationally recognized certification sets the competency standard for professionals leading AI projects across various industry sectors.
