STMIK Tazkia welcomed representatives from Politeknik IDN Bogor to discuss a strategic partnership in information technology education. The meeting, held at the STMIK Tazkia campus, marked the beginning of a collaboration between two IT higher education institutions in the Bogor area.

![STMIK Tazkia and Politeknik IDN Bogor Collaboration](/images/news/kerjasama-politeknik-idn-bogor-2026.jpeg)

## Synergy Between Two IT Campuses in Bogor

The collaboration between STMIK Tazkia and Politeknik IDN Bogor is driven by a shared commitment to strengthen their capacity in delivering quality information technology education. Both institutions share a common vision of producing competent IT graduates ready to face the challenges of the digital industry.

Politeknik IDN Bogor, as a vocational education institution in the technology field, brings strong experience in developing practice-based curricula. Meanwhile, STMIK Tazkia applies a [project-based learning](/en/programs) method that involves students directly in real-world projects with industry partners and social organizations.

This collaboration between the two institutions is expected to create synergies that enrich the learning experience for students from both campuses.

## Areas of Collaboration

During the meeting, both parties discussed several potential areas of cooperation:

### Knowledge and Resource Exchange

Both institutions agreed to share expertise and academic resources, including:
- Guest lecturer exchanges and speakers for public lectures
- Sharing of teaching materials and curricula in complementary fields
- Joint access to laboratory facilities and IT infrastructure

### Research and Development Collaboration

Collaboration in technology research and development was identified as a key focus:
- Joint research in artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and software development
- Development of appropriate technology projects for communities around Bogor
- Joint participation in national-level technology competitions

### Student Competency Development

Both campuses committed to enhancing student competencies through:
- Cross-institutional internship programs
- Joint workshops and seminars featuring industry practitioners
- Collaborative projects between students from both campuses

## Strengthening the IT Education Ecosystem in Bogor

This partnership is part of STMIK Tazkia's efforts to build a strong IT education ecosystem in the Bogor area. By collaborating with Politeknik IDN Bogor, both institutions can complement each other's strengths and deliver greater benefits to students and the community.

STMIK Tazkia, with its [Information Systems](/en/programs/information-systems) and [Computer Engineering](/en/programs/computer-engineering) programs, continues to expand its partnership network to ensure students receive education that is relevant to industry needs.
