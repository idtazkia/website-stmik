**STMIK Tazkia** contributed to the inauguration of the **Maryudi (Margajaya Yuk Digital)** Posyandu and **Dandelion** applications held in **Bogor Barat Sub-district, Bogor City**. This event was part of the effort to digitalize community health services at the sub-district level, particularly in monitoring child growth and stunting prevention.

![Inauguration of Maryudi Posyandu and Dandelion applications in Bogor Barat Sub-district](/images/news/denvi01.jpeg)

## About the Maryudi Posyandu Application

The **Maryudi (Margajaya Yuk Digital)** Posyandu application is a web-based health information system designed to support Posyandu (Integrated Health Service Post) operations in the Margajaya Village (RW 05) area of Bogor Barat Sub-district. This project was developed by a student of the [Computer Engineering](/en/programs/computer-engineering) program under the supervision of **Hairul Umam, S.Si., M.Si.** The application can be accessed at [https://posyandu-margajaya-rw05.netlify.app](https://posyandu-margajaya-rw05.netlify.app).

### Key Features

The Maryudi Posyandu application is equipped with various features to support health cadres and Posyandu workers:

- **Child medical record keeping** — including weight, height, upper arm circumference (MUAC), Vitamin A administration, and nutritional status
- **Nutritional status monitoring** — automatic classification into Normal, Alert, Danger, and Stunting categories
- **Analytics dashboard** — monthly examination trend charts, nutritional distribution, and RT (neighborhood) area mapping
- **Growth tracking** — comparison of child development over time
- **Attendance recording** — documentation of cadre attendance and task distribution
- **Bulk data upload** — importing data from Excel/CSV for efficient record-keeping
- **Role-based access** — different access levels for Admin, Cadre, Sub-district, and Residents

## Impact of Posyandu Digitalization

The digitalization of Posyandu services through the Maryudi application has a significant impact on the Margajaya Village community:

- **Early stunting detection** — regular nutritional status monitoring enables faster intervention
- **Record-keeping efficiency** — replacing error-prone manual recording with an integrated digital system
- **Data transparency** — child health data can be accessed by parents, cadres, and sub-district officials in real-time
- **Area mapping** — identification of stunting case concentrations by RT for more targeted interventions
