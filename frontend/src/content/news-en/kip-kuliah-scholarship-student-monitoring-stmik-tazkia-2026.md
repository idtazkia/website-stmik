**STMIK Tazkia** consistently holds **weekly monitoring sessions for KIP-Kuliah (Kartu Indonesia Pintar Kuliah) scholarship recipients** at the beginning of each week. Held at the STMIK Tazkia campus in Bogor, these sessions are led by **Wahyudin, S.Kom., M.E.** and aim to track the academic progress and well-being of scholarship students.

![KIP-Kuliah student monitoring session at STMIK Tazkia with speaker Wahyudin, S.Kom., M.E.](/images/news/monitoring-kip-kuliah-stmik-tazkia-01.jpeg)

## Weekly Monitoring Every Start of the Week

This monitoring program is a routine initiative conducted every beginning of the week by STMIK Tazkia. It serves as a structured platform for ongoing mentoring and evaluation of all KIP-Kuliah scholarship recipients. With a consistent schedule, students receive structured guidance throughout their academic journey.

During each monitoring session, **Wahyudin, S.Kom., M.E.** delivers material covering various essential aspects for scholarship recipients:

- **Academic performance evaluation** — tracking GPA, attendance, and credit completion progress
- **Character building and motivation** — strengthening students' mental resilience and learning enthusiasm
- **KIP-Kuliah policy updates** — sharing the latest information on scholarship regulations and requirements
- **Academic problem-solving support** — identifying and resolving challenges faced by students

![STMIK Tazkia KIP-Kuliah students attending the weekly monitoring session](/images/news/monitoring-kip-kuliah-stmik-tazkia-02.jpeg)

## Objectives and Benefits

The weekly monitoring program has several strategic objectives that support the success of KIP-Kuliah recipients:

### Maintaining Academic Standards

Regular monitoring ensures that KIP-Kuliah students meet the academic standards required by the scholarship program. This includes achieving the minimum GPA, maintaining lecture attendance, and completing assignments and examinations on time.

### Early Problem Detection

Through weekly meetings at the start of each week, the institution can identify early on various issues faced by students, both academic and non-academic. Preventive measures can be taken promptly before problems escalate.

### Building Community and Solidarity

The monitoring sessions also serve as an opportunity for KIP-Kuliah recipients across different cohorts and study programs to connect. This interaction builds a sense of togetherness and mutual support among scholarship recipients.

![Speaker Wahyudin, S.Kom., M.E. delivering monitoring material to KIP-Kuliah students](/images/news/monitoring-kip-kuliah-stmik-tazkia-03.jpeg)

## STMIK Tazkia's Commitment to KIP-Kuliah Students

STMIK Tazkia regards KIP-Kuliah recipients as an important part of the academic community that deserves special attention and guidance. This weekly monitoring program is one of the institution's concrete efforts to ensure every scholarship recipient can complete their studies successfully and on time.

In addition to monitoring, STMIK Tazkia provides various supporting facilities for KIP-Kuliah students, including access to the [SILINCAH application](/en/news/softlaunching-silincah-academic-service-system-stmik-tazkia-2026) for streamlined academic services and various competency development programs through [project-based learning](/en/programs) that characterizes the learning experience at STMIK Tazkia.
